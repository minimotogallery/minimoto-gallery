MM / POSITION / MURCIA

By the time consensus arrives, the position has already shifted.

MiniMoto Gallery is a contemporary art gallery based in Murcia. It works with artists at different stages of their careers, from first public presentations to established practices. Each collaboration begins with the work itself: what it needs in order to be shown precisely and where it might travel after the exhibition.

The space changes with every project. Installation, texts and the way visitors move through the gallery are decided in response to the works. Residencies and live activations are used when the project requires them, rather than as formats to be repeated on every occasion.

MiniMoto’s scale allows the gallery to work directly with artists. There is time to reconsider an installation, discuss a decision or return to a work without turning the process into a production line. Some relationships begin with a single exhibition; others continue and take different forms over time.

The gallery presents and documents projects, organises visits and introduces works to collectors, institutions and other professional spaces. An acquisition supports an artist’s future work, while also establishing a lasting relationship with the work and with the trajectory of the person who made it.

From the present, whatever moves ahead appears small.

MM / POSITION / MURCIA

By the time consensus arrives, the position has already shifted.

MiniMoto Gallery is a contemporary art gallery based in Murcia, in the Spanish Southeast. It works with practices that are still constructing how they appear, turning that unstable phase into exhibition, documentation and public circulation.

We do not understand the gallery as a neutral background. Each project may alter the space, the installation, the text and the circulation. Exhibitions, residencies and activations are different devices for the same task: producing the conditions a work requires without reducing it to a quick image.

We are interested in artists whose practice cannot be summarised as a style or category. MiniMoto’s scale allows for close reading, exact decisions and conversations that continue after an exhibition ends. Entering the gallery does not mean filling a slot, but joining a context that protects singularity and demands precision.

MiniMoto accompanies these practices as they move towards collections, institutions and new exhibition contexts. For collectors, acquisition is not an ending but a form of continuity: time, trust and the capacity for a practice to keep growing.

From the present, whatever moves ahead appears small.

MINIMOTO / MURCIA / POSITION

A gallery of precise scale.

MiniMoto works with artists whose practices do not need to be simplified in order to be seen.

01 / POSITION

Small does not mean provisional.

MiniMoto is a contemporary art gallery in Murcia. It produces exhibitions, residencies and activations through a close relationship with the works and with those who make them.

Scale allows precision: reading slowly, deciding carefully and preventing a practice from losing complexity when it becomes public.

02 / METHOD

Each project determines its own device.

We do not apply a fixed form. Space, installation, text and circulation are built from the internal logic of each work.

Curating also means producing conditions: time, interlocution, context and a public form that does not reduce the work to a quick image.

03 / ARTISTS

Entering MiniMoto is not filling a slot.

It means joining a demanding conversation. We are interested in practices with an internal logic, even when they have not yet found the place from which they can become legible.

The gallery accompanies that singularity without correcting it to fit a programme.

04 / COLLECTING

A collection distinguishes before consensus makes things obvious.

Collecting with MiniMoto means entering a practice early, understanding its context and participating in the continuity it needs in order to grow.

We work directly and discreetly with people and institutions who understand acquisition as a form of thought, rather than as a late confirmation.

About / MiniMoto Gallery

A gallery that is small only in scale.

MiniMoto Gallery is a contemporary art gallery in Murcia. It works with artists at different stages of their careers and builds each exhibition from the specific needs of the work.

MM / position

The exhibition as a way of working.

Each collaboration begins with the work itself: what it needs in order to be shown precisely, which decisions the installation requires and which context allows it to be read best.

The space changes with every project. Installation, texts and the way the room is navigated are decided from the works rather than from a repeated formula.

MiniMoto’s scale allows a direct relationship with artists. There is time to revise an installation, discuss a decision or return to a work without turning the process into a production line.

The gallery presents and documents projects, organises visits and connects works with collectors, institutions and other professional spaces when that route is relevant to the work.

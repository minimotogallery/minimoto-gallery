FOLIO I / WHAT MINIMOTO IS

A gallery of precise scale and disobedient ambition.

MiniMoto Gallery is a contemporary art gallery located in the Spanish Southeast. Its scale is not the result of limitation, but of a decision: to work close to the works, to those who produce them, and to the precise conditions that allow a practice to acquire a public form.

It does not operate as a neutral container or as a sequence of openings. Each project requires a reconsideration of space, language, accompaniment and the way a work may circulate. The gallery therefore appears as an instrument: small, mobile, precise, and able to alter its configuration whenever the practice demands it.

FOLIO II / THE METHOD

The work before the scene.

MiniMoto accompanies processes that require time, conversation and situated reading. It works through exhibitions, residencies, activations and movements between spaces, but does not apply the same formula to all of them. Production is developed alongside each artist and responds to the true scale of the proposal.

Installation, the editing of a text, the relationship with a territory or the choice of an interlocutor are all part of the curatorial work. They are not peripheral services: they form the exact place in which a practice decides how it wishes to be seen.

We do not ask a work to fit MiniMoto. We look for the form of MiniMoto that the work requires.

FOLIO III / THE ARTISTS

To belong here is to be read with attention.

Our relationship with artists is not built upon the promise of rapid visibility, but upon sustained involvement. We are interested in practices with an internal logic, even when they have not yet found the space in which they can become legible. MiniMoto works so that this singularity is neither corrected, diminished nor translated into a conventional language.

Each new presence expands the intelligence of the project. The gallery does not gather names to complete a list; it builds a constellation of affinities, frictions and differences capable of producing context. To be part of MiniMoto is to enter a demanding conversation and to share a responsibility: preserving the force of the works as they begin to circulate.

FOLIO IV / COLLECTING

A collection begins long before an acquisition.

For MiniMoto, collecting is not merely a matter of possessing a work. It means recognising a practice while it still retains risk, accompanying its development and assuming a position within the history that the work is beginning to write.

The gallery proposes direct, rigorous and discreet relationships with collectors, institutions and agents who understand acquisition as a form of thought. Each work is presented with the context required to understand not only what it is, but also what it may come to activate.

MiniMoto does not offer access to a trend. It offers proximity to a practice before its importance becomes evident.
